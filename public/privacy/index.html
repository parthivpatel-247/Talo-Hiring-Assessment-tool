Privacy notice

Last updated 20 October 2026

Talo is the assessment tool Hevo Data uses for campus hiring. This notice explains what happens to your information when your resume is shortlisted for a drive and when you take the test.

What we collect

FromWhat
Your resume, shared with us by you or your college placement officeThe resume file, and the details read from it: name, email, phone number, college, degree, graduation year, skills and projects.
Signing inYour email address, the access code you used, the time, your IP address and browser type. Failed and blocked sign-ins are recorded too.
The testYour answers, the language you chose, when each answer was saved, how long you took, and the questions you were given.
Test integrityWhen you left the test window, tab or full screen and for how long; when your connection dropped and for how long; attempts to copy or paste; and whether your answers closely match another candidate's.
EvaluationScores, grading notes and interview questions prepared for you, and the hiring team's decision.

We do not use your camera, microphone or screen recording, we do not track your location, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

  • To identify you, give you the right test and stop anyone else from taking it as you.
  • To evaluate your answers and decide who moves to the interview round.
  • To keep the assessment fair: integrity signals help the hiring team decide where to look more closely. They are never treated as proof on their own.
  • To keep the service secure and working (sign-in protection, error reports, usage counts).

We process this information because you take part in our recruitment process, and with the consent you give on the rules page before you start. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us; we will then stop evaluating your application.

AI and decisions

An AI service reads the text of your resume to fill in your details, and drafts a score for each answer against a written rubric. The hiring team can see the rubric, the draft score and the reasoning, and changes them where needed. People make every shortlisting and rejection decision; no decision about you is made by software alone.

Who processes it

We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers. These service providers process it on our behalf, only to run the assessment:

ProviderWhat forWhat they receive
CloudflareHosting, database, file storage, bot protection on sign-in formsEverything listed above, stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure
OpenAIReading resumes, drafting scores and interview questionsResume text and your written answers
Our email providerSending sign-in codes and invitationsYour name, email and the message

These providers may process data outside India. Within Hevo Data, only the people running the hiring drive can see your information, and every time someone opens a resume or exports results it is logged.

How long we keep it

Resumes, answers, scores, activity, exports and backups are deleted automatically 15 days after they are created, unless the law requires us to keep something longer. You can ask us to delete your information sooner (see below). A record that a privacy request was made and answered is kept for one year.

Security

  • Every connection is encrypted (HTTPS only).
  • Resumes are stored in private storage that is never publicly reachable.
  • The hiring team signs in with individual accounts, strong passwords and, when enabled, a second step by email. Accounts lock after repeated wrong passwords.
  • Access codes and passwords are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Answer keys and grading rubrics never reach your browser.

Cookies and storage

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is wrong or incomplete, such as your name or college.
  • Delete your information, which also withdraws your application for this drive.

You can also raise a concern or complaint with us at any time. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may contact the data protection authority where you live.

If something goes wrong

If a security incident affects your information, we will investigate at once, contain it, and inform you and the relevant authorities as the law requires, telling you what happened, what it means for you and what we are doing about it.

Contact and requests

Email the hiring team, or use this form. Use the email address that was on your resume so we can find your records.
