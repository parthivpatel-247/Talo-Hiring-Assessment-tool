Assessment terms

Last updated 20 October 2026

You agree to these terms when you tick the box on the rules page and start the test. They exist so every candidate is assessed fairly.

Who can take it

Only candidates invited by Hevo Data for a campus drive, using the email address on the resume we received. Each candidate gets one attempt. A second sign-in on another device is blocked while your test is running.

Your own work

  • Answer on your own, without help from other people.
  • Do not use notes, websites, search engines, AI assistants or code generators during the test.
  • Do not share the questions or your answers with anyone, during or after the test.

During the test

  • The test runs in full screen. If you leave it (another tab, app or window, or exit full screen), a countdown starts; if you don't come back in time, your test is submitted.
  • Copy, paste and right-click are turned off.
  • Reloading the page while the test is running ends it.
  • Leaving the test, losing connection and copy or paste attempts are recorded and shown to the hiring team, along with answers that closely match another candidate's.

Technical problems

Your answers save automatically. If your internet drops, the clock pauses and the test reconnects by itself; you continue where you left off. If something else goes wrong, tell the invigilator at once: they can resume your test with the same questions and your saved answers, or give you a fresh attempt where that is fair. Problems reported after you leave the hall may not be fixable.

Confidentiality

The questions, examples and test materials belong to Hevo Data and are confidential. You may not copy, record, photograph, publish or share them.

Your answers

Your answers remain your own work. You allow Hevo Data to store, read and evaluate them for this hiring process, as described in the privacy notice.

Acceptable use

Do not try to access another candidate's test, the admin area, answer keys or any part of the system you were not given, and do not interfere with how it works. Any such attempt ends your application and may be reported.

Outcome

Taking the assessment does not guarantee an interview or a job offer. If we find that these terms were broken, we may cancel the result. The hiring team reviews integrity signals in context before deciding, and you may be asked to explain them.

Changes and contact

We may update these terms before a future drive; the date at the top shows the latest version. Questions: contact the hiring team.
