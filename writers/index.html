About this assessment

This is a writing assessment in two timed parts. Write your own answer to each question. Each question shows its word limit. Write clearly and keep to the point.

Part 1 (4 questions, 30 minutes) is closed book:

  • Stay in full screen. If you switch tabs, windows or apps, you have 10 seconds to return before your assessment is submitted automatically.
  • Copying and pasting are turned off. Type your answers in your own words.
  • When Part 1 ends, it is locked. Once you submit Part 1, or its time runs out, you can't change those answers.

Part 2 (2 questions, 45 minutes) is open book:

  • You may switch tabs to look things up, and you may copy and paste.
  • Tab switches and pastes are recorded for the hiring team. The writing must be your own.
  • Part 2's timer starts when you select Start Part 2.

In both parts your answers save automatically. If your internet drops, the timer pauses and picks up where it left off once you are back online. Do not refresh or close this page while a part is running: refreshing ends your assessment.
